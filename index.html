RoadRisk AI helps users analyze road surfaces. The platform uses a vision classifier and a tabular risk model to detect cracks and estimate the condition of pavement. It processes data from weather reports and traffic signals to offer a complete view of road safety.

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 or equivalent
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 12 compatible card with 2 GB VRAM
  • Storage: 500 MB of space

  4. Open the folder where you saved the file.

⚙️ How to Run the Software

After you finish the installation, you can start the program using the shortcut on your desktop. When the window opens, the system will prompt you for input data. You can provide images or video files of road surfaces through the file browser inside the app. The AI model analyzes these files against live weather and traffic data. Results appear in the main dashboard as a risk score. This score helps you understand if the road needs repair or maintenance. The software saves these reports automatically in your documents folder for future review.

🛡️ Security and Privacy

This software runs locally on your machine. We do not upload your images or location data to external servers. All processing happens on your own hardware to keep your sensitive data secure and private. You remain in charge of all information collected during the analysis.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Does this app need an internet connection? Yes, the app requires a connection to fetch live weather and traffic updates. It does not send your road data over the internet.

Can I use it on other operating systems? Currently, we only support Windows. We may release versions for other systems in the future.

What if the app freezes during analysis? Large video files might take time to process. Please wait a few moments for the model to finish the calculation.
